02 — Research

What I work on

Machine learning for problems where the signal is weak, the data is scarce and the stakes are clinical.

Biomedical Signal Processing

Non-invasive fetal ECG extraction from abdominal recordings with diffusion models, dynamical priors and optimal transport. DST–SERB funded work on fetal arrhythmia.

Fetal ECGEEG / BCIDiffusion

Medical Image Analysis

Cardiovascular risk from fundus images and ECG, Alzheimer's classification on 3D MRI with Vision Mamba and ViTs, and topology-preserving segmentation.

FundusMRISegmentation

Few-shot & Anomaly Detection

Vision–language adapters with learnable text anchors for few-shot medical anomaly classification, built for settings with very few labelled cases.

CLIPFew-shotAnomaly

Infrared Sensing & Activity

Privacy-preserving activity and fall detection with low-resolution infrared array sensors and hybrid CNN–LSTM / CGAN models. The core of my Keio Ph.D.

IR arraysCNN–LSTMCGAN

Detection & Tracking

Drone detection and tracking, and radar tracking with neural combinatorial fusion and calibrated uncertainties, shipped through Shinkai Labs.

DronesRadarUncertainty

Edge AI & Industrial Vision

Models on Raspberry Pi and NVIDIA Jetson; crack, rust and fault detection for railway and facade inspection with Raspect and Alstom.
